Year ending March filters CSA charts. The National tab uses ABS financial years and is not filtered by LGA. Do not add CSA products together, and do not compare ABS unique offenders with CSA alleged offender incidents.

Use rates to compare LGAs. Melbourne leads recorded-offence counts as the activity centre; highest alleged-offender rate in 2026 is Latrobe, not Melbourne.

Alleged offender incidents are not unique people and are not proven offences. Youth is CSA ages 10–17. Sex shares use known sex only (Males / Females).

ABS Recorded Crime – Offenders counts unique alleged offenders proceeded against in a financial year. That is not CSA alleged offender incidents.

Sources: Crime Statistics Agency (Victoria), year ending March 2026, published 19 June 2026; Australian Bureau of Statistics, Recorded Crime – Offenders, 2024–25, published 18 March 2026. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. CSA offences, incidents and alleged offender incidents are separate official products. ABS unique offenders are a different product and period. This is an aggregate public-sector analytics dashboard. It does not identify individuals.
